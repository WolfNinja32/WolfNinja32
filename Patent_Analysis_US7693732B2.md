# Patent Analysis: US7693732B2
**Title:** Automatic information processing and electronic commerce system  
**Inventor:** [Your Name/Associate]  
**Filing Date:** August 1999  
**Issue Date:** April 2010  

---

## 🌐 The "Foundational" Context
This patent is considered foundational because it established a priority date for **automated network processing** in 1999—nearly seven years before the term "Cloud Computing" entered the mainstream lexicon (circa 2006). 

While the early internet was largely a "Read-Only" environment, this patent described a "Functional" environment: a framework where a central system could automatically process data and execute commerce tasks on behalf of distributed users.

### Key Innovations Predating the Cloud:
* **Utility Computing:** The shift from local software to server-side "Automatic Information Processing."
* **Scalable E-Commerce:** A blueprint for how web-based systems handle transactions and data management without manual intervention.
* **Network Intelligence:** Moving beyond simple data transmission to "intelligent" data handling—the precursor to modern **Edge Computing**.

---

## 📡 The Cable & Telecommunications Connection
The influence of this patent is particularly visible in the evolution of **Cable TV Providers** (MSOs - Multi-System Operators) into **Internet Service Providers (ISPs)**.

* **InterDigital Acquisition:** As part of the Tantivy Communications portfolio, this patent became a core asset for InterDigital, a leader in licensing foundational tech to the telecommunications industry.
* **Cloud-Based Services:** Cable giants like **Comcast (Xfinity)** and **Charter (Spectrum)** utilized the principles in this patent to transition from analog broadcasting to cloud-based On-Demand services and automated billing systems.
